About the Project

This project uses Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) to identify hate speech in online platforms.

Social media platforms have enabled hate speech and cyberbullying, posing risks to vulnerable groups.

Detection is difficult due to the sheer volume of user-generated content and the complexity of language.

We use RNN-based architectures to accurately detect and mitigate hate speech in social media.
